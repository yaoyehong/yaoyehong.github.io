## <span style="color: rgb(43, 93, 190);">Multiple fully funded PhD positions are available in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at The University of Hong Kong (HKU) for Fall 2027 and Spring 2028.</span>

We are seeking highly motivated and creative students to join our research group and pursue interdisciplinary research at the intersection of mechanics, materials science, advanced manufacturing, and robotics.



## <span style="color: rgb(43, 93, 190);">Research Areas</span>

Our research program focuses on three interconnected directions:
- **Physically Intelligent Soft Robotics**
- **Mechanical Metamaterials**
- **Smart Materials**

Our research aims to develop intelligent structures and robotic systems with programmable shape transformations, adaptive behaviors, and multifunctional capabilities, with broad applications in robotics, aerospace, and healthcare.
Research projects may involve:
- **Structural Design and Mechanics:** Mechanics-guided and AI-assisted design, elastic instabilities, multistability, analytical modeling, and numerical simulation.
- **Smart Materials and Advanced Manufacturing:** Materials synthesis and processing, advanced manufacturing (e.g., 3D printing), and soft actuation technologies, including pneumatic actuation and stimuli-responsive materials such as liquid crystal elastomers, magnetoelastomers, and hydrogels.
- **Soft Robotics and Functional Integration:** Design, fabrication, actuation, sensing, and control of soft robotic systems, including mechanical characterization and experimental testing.



## <span style="color: rgb(43, 93, 190);">Qualifications</span>

We welcome highly motivated candidates with experimental, analytical, or computational backgrounds.

Relevant disciplines include, but are not limited to:
- Solid mechanics and dynamics
- Mechanical and aerospace engineering
- Materials science and engineering
- Advanced manufacturing and 3D printing
- Robotics and control
- Electronics, sensors, and related fields
- Biomedical and chemical engineering
- Artificial intelligence and computational design

Candidates should demonstrate strong research interests, self-motivation, independent thinking, and a willingness to work across disciplines.

Applicants with a master's degree are preferred but not required. Candidates with strong experimental, computational, or analytical experience are particularly encouraged to apply.



## <span style="color: rgb(43, 93, 190);">Research Environment</span>

Our group integrates fundamental mechanics with computational and experimental research to explore emerging concepts in intelligent materials, structures, and robotic systems.

PhD students will have opportunities to:
- Conduct original and interdisciplinary research
- Develop theoretical, computational, and experimental skills
- Participate in international research collaborations
- Present research at international conferences
- Publish research findings in leading scientific journals
- Develop independent research skills for future academic or industrial careers



## <span style="color: rgb(43, 93, 190);">Funding and Scholarships</span>

Admitted PhD students will receive **full financial support through The University of Hong Kong (HKU)**, subject to university admission and scholarship requirements.
We strongly encourage outstanding applicants to apply for the **Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme (HKPFS)** and will actively support competitive candidates throughout the application process.
For more information, please visit the [HKU Graduate School – Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme](https://gradsch.hku.hk/prospective_students/fees_scholarships_and_financial_support/hong_kong_phd_fellowship_scheme).



## <span style="color: rgb(43, 93, 190);">Postdoctoral, Visiting Scholar, and Research Assistant Opportunities</span>

We also welcome inquiries from **postdoctoral researchers, visiting scholars, and research assistants** whose research interests align with our group's research directions.
For outstanding postdoctoral candidates with strong research records and demonstrated research potential, we will actively support applications for competitive fellowships, including:
- RGC Junior Research Fellow Scheme (JRFS)
- Hong Kong Scholars Program (香江学者计划)
- Other relevant postdoctoral fellowship and talent development programs

**Visiting scholars** interested in research collaborations and **prospective research assistants** with relevant technical or research experience are also encouraged to contact us.

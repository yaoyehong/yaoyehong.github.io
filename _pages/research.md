# Research

Our research focuses on the mechanics-guided design of intelligent materials, structures, and robotics.

By integrating solid mechanics, materials science, advanced manufacturing, and robotics, we seek to understand how geometry, topology, and material properties can be engineered to achieve programmable shape transformations, adaptive behaviors, and multifunctional capabilities.

Our research spans three interconnected areas.

---

## 1. Physically Intelligent Soft Robotics

We develop soft robotic systems that leverage material properties, structural geometry, and mechanical instabilities to achieve physically intelligent behaviors.

Our research explores:

- Multimodal locomotion and adaptive mobility
- Programmable shape morphing and robotic reconfiguration
- Snap-through-enabled actuation and rapid motion
- Bioinspired soft actuators and robotic systems
- Environmental interaction and functional integration

Our goal is to develop soft robotic systems capable of adapting to complex environments while reducing reliance on sophisticated control systems.

---

## 2. Shape-Morphing Mechanical Metamaterials

We investigate the fundamental mechanics and design of programmable structures that exhibit complex shape transformations and mechanical responses.

Our research focuses on:

- Elastic instabilities and snap-through transitions
- Multistability and programmable morphogenesis
- Geometry- and topology-guided structural design
- Mechanics-based modeling and inverse design
- Multifunctional and reconfigurable metamaterials

By understanding the coupling between geometry, deformation, and mechanical instability, we aim to establish predictive design principles for adaptive and shape-morphing structures.

---

## 3. Smart and Active Materials

We explore stimuli-responsive materials and their integration into soft actuators, artificial muscles, and intelligent robotic systems.

Our research includes:

- Liquid crystal elastomers (LCEs)
- Magnetically responsive soft materials
- Stimuli-responsive actuation and shape programming
- Multifunctional artificial muscles
- Integration of active materials with programmable structures

Our goal is to develop intelligent material systems with programmable actuation, sensing, and adaptive mechanical responses.

---

## Research Methods

Our research integrates theoretical, computational, and experimental approaches, including:

- Analytical modeling and nonlinear mechanics
- Finite element analysis (FEA)
- Mechanics-guided and AI-assisted design
- Advanced manufacturing and 3D printing
- Smart material synthesis and processing
- Mechanical characterization and robotic testing

# Welcome!

I am **Yaoye Hong**, an incoming Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at **The University of Hong Kong (HKU)**.

My research focuses on the mechanics-guided design of **physically intelligent soft robotic systems, shape-morphing mechanical metamaterials, and smart and active materials**.

By integrating mechanics, materials science, advanced manufacturing, and robotics, my research aims to develop intelligent materials, structures, and robotic systems with programmable shape transformations, adaptive behaviors, and multifunctional capabilities.

## Research Interests

My research focuses on three interconnected areas:

### Physically Intelligent Soft Robotics

Developing soft robotic systems with programmable locomotion, adaptive shape transformations, and environmental interactions.

### Shape-Morphing Mechanical Metamaterials

Exploring elastic instabilities, multistability, and geometry-guided structural design to achieve programmable and multifunctional mechanical behaviors.

### Smart and Active Materials

Developing soft actuators and artificial muscles using liquid crystal elastomers, magnetoelastomers, and other stimuli-responsive materials.
